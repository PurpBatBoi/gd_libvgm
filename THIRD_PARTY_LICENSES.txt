READ ME FIRST  (this part is for you; you do not have to ship it)
==============================================================================

This file collects the copyright notices and license texts of everything that
is compiled into the extension: the sound-chip emulation cores, the resampler,
zlib, godot-cpp and the extension itself. Every one of these licenses
(BSD-3-Clause, MIT, ISC, zlib) allows commercial use and does not make you
release your game's source code. In exchange they ask you to keep the
copyright notice and the license text with the software.

What to do: ship everything from the line "BEGIN NOTICES" to the end of this
file with your game, for example as a text file next to the executable, on a
credits or licenses screen, or in the game's documentation. The BSD-3-Clause
license (the one most cores use) asks for this explicitly for binary
distributions. You may leave out the notices of cores you removed from your
own build; if you ship the extension as it is, include all of them.

NOT covered by this file (check these yourself):

   1. The libvgm framework itself (player/, emu/*.c, the *intf.c chip
   interface files and utils/ of the bundled libvgm -
   https://github.com/ValleyBell/libvgm by Valley Bell and contributors). The
   bundled copy has no license file and no license headers, so this file
   cannot grant any rights to it. That has to be settled with its author
   before you release a game. It is the only part of the extension that is not
   under a license listed below.

   2. Godot Engine itself. It is not part of the extension. Its own license
   and the licenses of its third-party components are listed by the engine
   (Help > About Godot > Third-party Licenses in the editor, or the Godot
   website), and you have to credit them separately.

   3. Data you add: music files (VGM, S98, ...) have their own copyright. ROM
   files you give the extension (for example yrw801.rom for the YMF278B, or
   your own YM2608 rhythm ROM) are not included in it and are not covered
   here.

To keep this file correct: when a sound core is added to or removed from
thirdparty/libvgm/emu/cores, update the table and the copyright lines below to
match.
